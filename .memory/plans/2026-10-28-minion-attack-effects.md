# Plan: Minion Attack Effects

## Goal

Make minion attacks readable and weighty for both sides: the attacker lunges toward its target, a weapon trail follows the card's visual theme, and a strong hit response distinguishes minion damage from hero damage. Preserve the existing combat action order and pause behavior.
