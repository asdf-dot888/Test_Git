# Decisions: Minion Attack Effects

- Date: 2026-10-28
- Status: Confirmed

## D01. Attack motion
- **Chosen**: For minion attacks, animate the attacking minion portrait with a short lunge toward its target, add a weapon-trail arc, and finish with a bright impact flash.
- **Rationale**: Make each attack read as a deliberate action with a clear path and impact.

## D02. Attack coverage and theme
- **Chosen**: Apply the effect to both player and enemy minion attacks. Keep one shared attack style and color it to match the card's existing visual theme. Hero attacks are outside this change.
- **Rationale**: Keep combat rules visually symmetric while retaining the identity of each card.

## D03. Damage feedback
- **Chosen**: Use a strong red blood-splatter effect and screen shake on minion damage. For hero damage, use a red screen flash and screen shake without blood splatter.
- **Rationale**: Make damage feel impactful while distinguishing damage to a minion from damage to a hero.
