# Decisions: Battle Layout and Audio Access

- Date: 2026-10-28
- Status: Confirmed

## D01. Hand and battlefield proportions
- **Chosen**: Reduce the hand card display moderately while keeping card details readable, and use the recovered screen space to enlarge the battlefield.
- **Rationale**: The battlefield is the main play area and should receive more of the expanded game screen without making hand cards hard to read.

## D02. Unavailable friendly minion appearance and targeting
- **Chosen**: Show friendly minions that cannot currently attack (summoned this turn or already attacked) with a gray, unavailable appearance. Keep them selectable as targets for healing or buff spells; prevent their attack and all-attack actions as before.
- **Rationale**: Make attack readiness clear without removing valid spell interactions.

## D03. All-attack control placement
- **Chosen**: Move the all-attack button to the upper-right area of the battlefield, with a sword icon and its existing localized label. Preserve its existing availability rules and sequential attack behavior.
- **Rationale**: Keep this field action visually distinct from the turn-end control and place it beside the action area it affects.

## D04. Audio settings access outside combat
- **Chosen**: Add an audio/settings button in the upper-right area of both the main menu and expedition roadmap. Open the existing audio-settings modal from either screen and reuse the existing persisted audio settings.
- **Rationale**: Let players adjust sound before starting or resuming combat without duplicating settings controls or storage.
